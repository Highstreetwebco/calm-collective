About this website

This website provides introductory information about Calm Collective and its proposed professional room-hire offering in Warwick. It is intended for independent practitioners considering space in which to meet their own clients.

Calm Collective does not provide therapy

Calm Collective provides rooms; it does not provide therapy through this website, diagnose or treat any condition, recommend a particular practitioner, or accept therapy appointments from members of the public. Practitioners using the rooms operate independently and are responsible for the services they provide to their own clients.

Enquiries are not bookings

Submitting a form, receiving a callback or attending a viewing does not reserve a room or create a room-hire agreement. Any hire is subject to suitability, availability, verification of relevant requirements and separate written terms agreed before use.

Practitioner responsibilities

Practitioners remain responsible for their qualifications, professional registrations, insurance, safeguarding, data protection, client records, consents, risk assessments and compliance with the rules applying to their particular service. Final eligibility requirements will be confirmed before any room-hire agreement.

Quiet-use requirement

The premises are intended for quiet, appointment-based professional sessions. Activities that create significant noise, disruption or high footfall may not be suitable. Calm Collective may ask for further information about a proposed service before discussing room hire.
