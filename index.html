A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you
settle in.

Ask us a question
Can I book therapy here?

Calm Collective hires rooms to independent practitioners. To book a therapy appointment, contact your practitioner directly.

Can I hire a room occasionally or every week?

Tell us whether you need occasional sessions, a regular weekly slot or a longer-term arrangement. We’ll discuss suitability, availability and terms with you.

How much does room hire cost?

Request the arrangement you need and we’ll talk through the relevant rates and terms before you commit.

When is my booking confirmed?

A request is not a confirmed booking. The team will check availability and suitability and contact you to agree the details. Please wait for confirmation before arranging clients.

What facilities are included?

The planned shared facilities include a waiting area, Wi-Fi, a kitchenette and a bathroom. Final facilities and individual room specifications will be confirmed before opening.

Is there step-free access and parking?

Contact us about your access requirements before booking. Full access, parking and arrival details will be confirmed as the premises are completed.

What professional requirements apply?

Requirements will be confirmed before a room-hire agreement. Each practitioner is responsible for their qualifications, registrations, insurance and professional obligations.

How do I change or cancel a request?

Call 07508 070295 or email calmcollectivebooking@gmail.com with your request reference. Cancellation terms for room hire will be agreed before booking.
