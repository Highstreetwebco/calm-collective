Nestled in the heart of Warwick, The Calm Collective offers a peaceful and welcoming space for therapists, wellbeing professionals and holistic practitioners to support their clients.

Our three thoughtfully designed therapy rooms provide a quiet, professional and comfortable environment where meaningful conversations can unfold and both you and your clients can feel truly at ease. The shared waiting area offers a warm and inviting welcome, helping clients feel comfortable from the moment they arrive.

Whether you are just beginning your journey as a practitioner or looking to grow an established practice, our flexible room hire options offer you the freedom to work in a way that suits you.

Each room is neutrally decorated to create a sense of calm and tranquillity, with the flexibility to personalise the space to suit your individual needs.

Ask us a question
Can I book therapy here?

Calm Collective hires rooms to independent practitioners. To book a therapy appointment, contact your practitioner directly.

Can I hire a room occasionally or every week?

Tell us whether you need occasional sessions, a regular weekly slot or a longer-term arrangement. We’ll discuss suitability, availability and terms with you.

How much does room hire cost?

All three rooms are £15 per hour. Solace is £45 for a half day (4 hours) or £80 for a full day (8 hours). Calm is £40 for up to 4 hours or £70 for up to 8 hours. Haven is £40 for 4 hours or £70 for 8 hours. Explore the rooms and prices.

Can I run a small group session?

Yes. Solace is suitable for small group sessions of up to 6 people, including guided relaxation, meditation and sound baths. Tell us about your session when you enquire so we can discuss the arrangement.

When is my booking confirmed?

A request is not a confirmed booking. The team will check availability and suitability and contact you to agree the details. Please wait for confirmation before arranging clients.

What facilities are included?

A warm, welcoming shared waiting area helps clients feel comfortable when they arrive. Ask us about the facilities and equipment available for your chosen room.

Is there step-free access and parking?

Contact us about your access requirements before booking. We’ll discuss access, parking and arrival details with you so you can check the space works for you and your clients.

What professional requirements apply?

Requirements will be confirmed before a room-hire agreement. Each practitioner is responsible for their qualifications, registrations, insurance and professional obligations.

How do I change or cancel a request?

Call 07508 070295 or email calmcollectiverooms@gmail.com with your request reference. Cancellation terms for room hire will be agreed before booking.
